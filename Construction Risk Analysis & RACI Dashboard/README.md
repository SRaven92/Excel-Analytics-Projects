# ⚠️ Construction Risk Analysis & RACI Dashboard

## Overview

An Excel-based risk management system I developed to bring construction and design risks, mitigation measures, residual risk and responsibility allocation into one structured workbook.

The system combines a traditional risk register with dashboard reporting and a linked RACI matrix, making it easier to see **which risks need attention, whether controls reduce the risk, and who is responsible for taking action**.

The portfolio version contains 60 fictional risk scenarios covering design, construction, fire, environmental, operational and project risks.

## Risk Analysis

Risk is assessed using a 5 × 5 methodology:

**Severity × Likelihood = Risk Rating**

Both **initial risk** and **residual risk after controls** are calculated, allowing the effect of mitigation measures to be compared.

### Risk Bands

| Score | Classification |
|---|---|
| 20–25 | 🔴 High |
| 10–19 | 🟠 Medium |
| 1–9 | 🟢 Low |

The workbook also uses RAG status separately from the numerical risk rating to track the progress of risk actions.

## Dashboard

The dashboard provides analysis across:

- Initial risk
- Residual risk
- Risk category
- RAG status
- Responsibility
- Open risks
- Overall risk profile

## RACI Responsibility Matrix

Risk-control actions are linked to a RACI matrix using:

- **R** – Responsible
- **A** – Accountable
- **C** – Consulted
- **I** – Informed

This provides a clearer link between identifying a risk and establishing who needs to do something about it.

## Portfolio Data

The **workbook structure, formulas, risk calculations, dashboard, RACI logic and reporting methodology were designed and built by me**.

For the public portfolio version, **AI was used to generate 60 fictional risk scenarios and supporting example data**. This allows the workbook to demonstrate realistic behaviour across different risk categories without using actual project, client or commercially sensitive information.

All example projects, organisations, contacts, hazards, controls, responsibilities and risk outcomes are synthetic.

> **Important:** This workbook is a portfolio demonstration and does not constitute an approved project risk assessment. Risk scores, controls, legislation and standards references are illustrative and require competent project-specific review and verification before practical use.
