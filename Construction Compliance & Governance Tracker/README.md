# 🏗️ Construction Compliance & Governance Tracker

## Overview

An Excel-based portfolio management system I developed to bring construction project, compliance, programme and governance information into one structured reporting environment.

The workbook is designed for managing multiple concurrent projects and makes it easier to see **what is live, what is overdue, where compliance information is missing, who is responsible and which projects require attention**.

The portfolio version contains **70 fictional projects** and demonstrates how a large project dataset can be transformed into practical dashboards, management views and action-based reporting.

## What It Tracks

- Project and client information
- Project status and RAG rating
- Actual and planned progress
- Schedule variance
- F10 dates and status
- PCIP progress
- Risk Register reviews
- Construction Phase Plan status
- Building Control appointments
- Building Regulations uploads and validation
- Fire and compliance information
- Health & Safety File progress
- Regulation 38 information
- Outstanding actions and review notes
- Project responsibility and contact information

## Dashboard & Reporting

The workbook includes portfolio-level reporting for:

- Live, pending, on-hold and closed projects
- Projects ahead of target, on target or behind schedule
- Building Control appointments
- Programme and schedule variance
- Open actions and compliance gaps
- Client and portfolio distribution
- Project responsibilities and workloads

Project performance is assessed by comparing actual completion against planned progress, with a tolerance used to distinguish between minor variation and projects that genuinely require attention.

## Portfolio Data

The **workbook structure, formulas, calculations, dashboards, reporting logic and governance workflow were designed and built by me**.

For the public portfolio version, **AI was used to generate fictional example data across 70 projects**. This allows the workbook to demonstrate realistic portfolio reporting and varied project scenarios without exposing real clients, projects, colleagues or commercially sensitive information.

All project names, organisations, contacts, dates, responsibilities, statuses, review notes and compliance outcomes are synthetic.

> Regulatory references and example compliance information are included to demonstrate how this type of data can be structured and reported. They should be independently checked against current legislation, guidance and project-specific requirements before practical use.
